## Content Area 1: Mechanics (Chapters 1 – 3)

### Chapter 1: Rotational Dynamics and Oscillatory Motion
- **Official Textbook Title:** Rotational Dynamics and Oscillatory Motion
- **Display Title:** Rotational Dynamics
- **Content Area:** Mechanics
- **Textbook Start Page:** Page 1 (CDC First Edition 2025)
- **Source Document:** *Physics Grade 12 (2025)*, Chapter 1, pp. 1–24
- **Subtopics:**
  1.1 Equations of angular motion with constant angular acceleration  
  1.2 Analogies between linear and rotational kinematic and dynamic quantities  
  1.3 Kinetic energy of rotation of a rigid body  
  1.4 Moment of inertia ($I$) and radius of gyration ($k$)  
  1.5 Parallel axes theorem ($I = I_{\text{cm}} + Md^2$) and perpendicular axes theorem ($I_z = I_x + I_y$)  
  1.6 Moments of inertia of regular bodies (uniform rod, ring, disc, solid cylinder, solid sphere)  
  1.7 Torque and angular acceleration relation ($\tau = I\alpha$)  
  1.8 Work, power, and energy in rotational motion ($W = \tau\theta$, $P = \tau\omega$)  
  1.9 Conservation of angular momentum ($\vec{L} = I\vec{\omega}$)  
  1.10 Rolling motion without slipping on an inclined plane ($K_{\text{total}} = \frac{1}{2}Mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$)  
- **Learning Objectives:**
  - *Official CDC Outcomes:* State analogies between linear and rotational physical quantities; define moment of inertia and radius of gyration; apply parallel and perpendicular axis theorems; apply the principle of conservation of angular momentum; evaluate acceleration of bodies rolling down an inclined plane.
  - *Textbook-Derived Focus:* Derivations of moments of inertia of uniform rods; kinetic energy partition in rolling bodies ($a = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + k^2/R^2}$).
  - *Physics Lab Synthesized Objectives:* Race simulation of sphere, cylinder, and ring down an inclined plane showing rotational energy distribution.
- **Key Equations:**
  $$\tau = I\alpha, \quad L = I\omega = \text{constant (if } \tau_{\text{ext}} = 0)$$
  $$I = I_{\text{cm}} + M d^2, \quad a_{\text{rolling}} = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + k^2/R^2}$$
- **Practical Activities:**
  - Determination of the moment of inertia of a flywheel.

### Chapter 2: Periodic Motion
- **Official Textbook Title:** Periodic Motion
- **Display Title:** Periodic Motion
- **Content Area:** Mechanics
- **Textbook Start Page:** Page 25 (CDC First Edition 2025)

- **Subtopics:**

  2.2 Kinematics of SHM: displacement, velocity, acceleration as sinusoidal functions  
  2.3 Reference circle and projection of uniform circular motion  
  2.4 Differential equation of linear SHM: $\frac{d^2 x}{dt^2} + \omega^2 x = 0$  
  2.5 Kinetic, potential, and total energy in SHM  
  2.6 Horizontal and vertical spring-mass systems  
  2.7 Simple pendulum time period ($T = 2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$)  
  2.8 Angular simple harmonic motion (torsional pendulum)  
  2.9 Free, damped, and forced oscillations; mechanical resonance  
- **Learning Objectives:**
  - *Official CDC Outcomes:* Define simple harmonic motion; solve the SHM differential equation; analyze kinetic and potential energy transformations during oscillation; derive time periods for spring-mass and simple pendulum; explain damped oscillations and resonance.

- **Key Equations:**
  $$\frac{d^2 x}{dt^2} + \omega^2 x = 0, \quad x(t) = A\sin(\omega t + \phi)$$
  $$T_{\text{spring}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}, \quad T_{\text{pendulum}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}, \quad E_{\text{total}} = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$$

- **Display Title:** Fluid Mechanics

- **Textbook Start Page:** Page 45 (CDC First Edition 2025)

  3.2 Archimedes' principle and laws of floatation  
  3.3 Surface tension, surface energy, angle of contact, and capillarity  
  3.4 Capillary rise equation ($h = \frac{2T\cos\theta}{r\rho g}$) and excess pressure in drops/bubbles

  3.6 Bernoulli's equation and applications (Venturimeter, efflux velocity, aerodynamic lift)
