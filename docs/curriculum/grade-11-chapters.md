- **Official Textbook Title:** Physical Quantities
- **Display Title:** Physical Quantities
- **Content Area:** Mechanics
- **Textbook Start Page:** Page 1 (CDC First Edition 2024)
- **Source Document:** *Physics Grade 11 (2024)*, Chapter 1, pp. 1–20
- **Subtopics:**
  1.1 Introduction to physics and physical quantities  
  1.2 Fundamental and derived units (SI system)  
  1.3 Dimensions and dimensional formulas of physical quantities  
  1.4 Principle of homogeneity of dimensions  
  1.5 Uses of dimensional equations (checking correctness, deriving relations, converting units)  
  1.6 Limitations of dimensional analysis

  1.8 Significant figures and rounding off rules in calculations  
- **Learning Objectives:**
  - *Official CDC Outcomes:* Relate physical quantities to SI base and derived units; express quantities in dimensional formulas $[M^a L^b T^c I^d]$; apply the principle of homogeneity to test validity of physical equations; estimate measurement uncertainties.

- **Key Equations:**
  $$[Q] = [M^a L^b T^c I^d]$$
  $$\frac{\Delta Z}{Z} = \sqrt{\left(a \frac{\Delta A}{A}\right)^2 + \left(b \frac{\Delta B}{B}\right)^2} \quad \text{for } Z = A^a B^b$$
- **Practical Activities:**
  - Measurement of dimensions using Vernier calipers.
  - Measurement of wire diameter and sheet thickness using a micrometer screw gauge.
  - Measurement of radius of curvature of spherical surfaces using a spherometer.
